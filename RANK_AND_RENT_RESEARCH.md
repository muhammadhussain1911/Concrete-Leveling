## 1. Executive Summary & The Economics of Concrete Leveling

The **Rank and Rent** business model is one of the highest-margin, lowest-churn vehicles in digital real estate. Unlike traditional client SEO (where you sell consulting hours to a business that owns the digital assets and can fire you once ranked), **Rank and Rent establishes you as the digital landlord**:
1. **You own the asset:** The domain, static web architecture, local rankings, Google Business Profile (GBP), phone tracking numbers, and conversion funnels belong 100% to you.
2. **The contractor is a tenant:** The contractor leases the inbound calls and form submissions exclusively on a fixed monthly retainer ($750 – $2,500/mo) or a Pay-Per-Lead (PPL) structure ($45 – $85/lead).
3. **Total leverage:** If a contractor stops paying, acts unprofessional, or misses appointments, you simply swap the call routing number and email forwarding address in `src/config/site.ts` to their top competitor in under 60 seconds.

### Why Concrete Leveling (Polyjacking) is a S-Tier Rank-and-Rent Niche
* **Average Job Ticket:** $1,800 to $4,500 for residential driveways and pool decks; $5,000 to $25,000+ for commercial warehouse slabs and municipal sidewalks.
* **Profit Margins:** 65% to 80%. A standard 2-man polyjacking crew can complete 3 to 4 residential lifting jobs per day using high-density expandable polyurethane foam, netting $2,000 to $5,000+ per rig per day.
* **Urgency & Buying Trigger:** Uneven concrete slabs are non-negotiable liabilities. Municipal code enforcement and HOAs issue trip hazard citations (ADA compliance violations), and home sales require sunken walkways and garage aprons to be repaired prior to title transfer.
* **Cost Disparity Advantage:** Polyurethane lifting costs 50% to 70% *less* than complete slab tear-out and replacement, with zero downtime (ready for vehicle traffic in 15 minutes). Homeowners actively search for alternatives to expensive $12,000 concrete replacement.
* **Search Volume vs. High Intent:** While general search volume is moderate (300 to 1,500 searches/mo per metro area), commercial intent is virtually 100%. People do not browse concrete leveling for entertainment—when they search, they need an estimate immediately.
